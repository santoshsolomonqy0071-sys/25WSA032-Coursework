Temperature Data Analysis Discussion

Plot 1 - Temperature vs Time 
The temperature reamined stable throughout the recording, staying between 20.48 and 20.69 degree C.
This shows the room temperature was consistent with only minor fluctuations. 



Plot 2 - Magnitude vs Frequency 
The DFT shows the dominant frequency was 0.98 Hz. Most of the magnitude values are low, showing that the temperature signal has little periodic variation



Plot 3 - Smoothed Temperature vs Time 
The moving average smooths out the small fluctuations seen in Plot 1, making the overall stable trend clearer. 



Plot 4 - Histograms of Temperatures
Most readings clustered around 20.51 degree C, confirming the temperature was stable. The spread was narrow, from 20.48 to 20.69 degree C. 



Plot 5 - Temperature Change Rate vs Time
The change rate stays close to zero throughout, with occasional small spikes. This confirms temperature was not changing significantly over time.
